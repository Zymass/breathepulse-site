BreathePulse

A tactile breathing pacer. Pick a rhythm and feel each breath through gentle haptics — no sound, no screen needed.

Privacy Policy Support

What it is

Most breathing apps ask you to watch an animation. BreathePulse taps the rhythm straight into your hand: a rising pulse as you breathe in, a steady touch while you hold, a fading pulse as you breathe out. You can run a whole session with the screen off.

Three rhythms

Box (4-4-4-4), Relaxing 4-7-8 for a long slow exhale, and Coherent (an even 5.5 in, 5.5 out). Choose how many cycles you want and the timer counts you down.

See your practice

The History tab shows minutes paced today, your last seven days, and recent sessions. A Home Screen Widget keeps today's minutes a glance away, and a Lock Screen Live Activity shows the current phase while you breathe.

Private by design

Zero data leaves your device. No account, no cloud, no analytics, no third-party SDKs. The whole app works fully offline.
